About

One accountant,two professions,three languages

William Lai & Co was founded in Sheffield in 2007 by William Lai, who has worked in accountancy for over 36 years.

Before setting up the practice, William worked for a large PLC and in the property investment and construction industry. That background still shapes the firm: many clients are landlords, developers and builders, and complicated tax problems are dealt with promptly and in plain language.

He reads and writes Chinese and English, speaks fluent Cantonese and English and good Putonghua, so Chinese-speaking clients can talk through their affairs without a language barrier.
