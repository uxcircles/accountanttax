Privacy & legal

Who we are, how we handle your information, and the small print.

About us

This website is run by William Lai, trading as William Lai & Co International Accountants, 24 Darfield Avenue, Sheffield S20 6SU. William Lai is a member of the Association of International Accountants (AIA) and practises under an AIA practising certificate. He is also a member of the Hong Kong Institute of Certified Public Accountants (HKICPA). Professional indemnity insurance details are available on request.

Privacy notice

William Lai & Co is the data controller for personal information you give us. Contact: wlai@accountanttax.co.uk.

What we collect and why

  • Enquiries. Your name, contact details and message, so we can reply. Legal basis: legitimate interests.
  • Client work. The financial and personal information needed to prepare accounts and tax returns. Legal basis: performing our contract with you, and legal obligations.
  • Anti-money-laundering checks. Proof of identity and address, as the Money Laundering Regulations 2017 require. Legal basis: legal obligation.

Who we share it with

HMRC, Companies House and other bodies when you ask us to file on your behalf, our professional body or insurers where required, and software providers who process data for us under contract. We never sell your information.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don’t lead to work: up to 12 months. Client records: 6 years after the end of our engagement, or longer where the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

This website

This site sets no cookies and uses no tracking. The contact form doesn’t send anything to a server: it opens your own email app.

Disclaimer

The guides and calculators on this site are general information for the UK tax year shown, not advice for your circumstances. Please talk to us before acting on them.
